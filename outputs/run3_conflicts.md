Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) Research Brief

Assumed Date:23 September 2026

1.Snapshot

Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (Q1 FY27), the company reported a Profit after Tax (PAT) of ₹82 crore, an increase of 5.9% year-on-year [S3]. Revenue from operations was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year-on-year [S3], although one news report stated revenue was ₹1,428 crore, representing a 35% increase [S1]. Operating (EBITDA) margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2%, primarily due to a sharp rise in copper prices [S1, S3]. Demand from power utilities and data-centre projects remains strong [S3].

2.Bull Case

Growing Demand & Order Book:The company reports strong demand from power transmission and data-centre projects [S3]. Data-centre cables constitute approximately 9% of domestic revenue and management expects this to double within two years [S2]. The order book increased to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S3].
Capacity Expansion:A new plant in Bharuch, Gujarat, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 (October-December 2026) [S3]. This plant will add approximately 30% to the company's cable capacity [S3] and is expected to contribute from the second half of FY27 [S2]. About 70% of the total ₹620 crore capex for Bharuch has already been spent, funded primarily by internal accruals and some term debt [S2].
Expected Margin Recovery:While Q1 FY27 margins were impacted by rising copper prices, management expects to pass on these costs to customers over the next one to two quarters, as roughly two-thirds of contracts include price-variation clauses [S2, S3].
Healthy Financial Position:The company maintains a net debt to equity ratio of 0.35 times [S2].
Decreasing Promoter Pledge:The percentage of promoter holding pledged with lenders has significantly decreased to 4.1% as of June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025 and 6.0% in March 2026 [S8]. This addresses older concerns raised in 2024 regarding promoter pledging [S4].
Export Growth:Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3].

3.Bear Case

Margin Pressure from Raw Materials:Operating margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, largely due to a nearly 14% increase in copper prices during the quarter [S1, S3]. While management anticipates passing on these costs, there is a reported lag of one to two quarters for price-variation clauses to reset [S2].
Rising Receivable Days:Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the past year, primarily due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. While the company is monitoring this and has not seen any defaults, it could impact working capital [S2].
GST Demand Order:The company has received a demand order for ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) related to input tax credit for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. Although Sarvottam Cables Ltd believes it has a strong case and intends to appeal, this represents a potential liability until resolved [S7].
Conflicting Revenue Figures:There is a notable discrepancy in reported Q1 FY27 revenue figures. The official company press release states ₹1,248 crore (18.0% YoY growth) [S3], while a news report indicates ₹1,428 crore (35% YoY growth) [S1]. This significant difference could cause investor confusion.

4.Open Questions

Revenue Discrepancy Resolution:Why do the official company press release [S3] and a major business news outlet [S1] report different revenue figures for Q1 FY27? Clarification is needed on the correct, definitive figure.
Impact of GST Demand:What is the estimated timeline for the appeal process regarding the ₹46.3 crore GST demand, and what is the potential financial impact if the appeal is unsuccessful [S7]?
Detailed Working Capital Management:What specific actions is the company taking to address the increase in receivable days, particularly with large state utility customers, and what is the expected timeline for improvement [S2]?
Unaudited vs. Audited Figures:While common for quarterly results, it is not explicitly stated if the Q1 FY27 figures are unaudited [S1, S3].
