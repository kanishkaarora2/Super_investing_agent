Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) - Research Brief

Assumed Current Date: 23 September 2026

1.Snapshot

Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (Q1 FY27), the company reported a net profit of ₹82 crore [S1, S3]. Revenue from operations was stated as either ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY) by a news outlet [S1] or ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) in the company's press release [S3]. Operating/EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% [S1, S3], primarily due to a nearly 14% rise in copper costs, its main raw material [S1, S3].

2.Bull case

Strong Demand and Order Book:Management notes strong demand from power transmission and data-centre projects [S3]. The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, an increase from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S3].
Revenue Growth & Guidance:The company reported significant revenue growth for Q1 FY27 (either +18% [S3] or +35% [S1]) and maintained its FY27 full-year revenue growth guidance of 15-17% [S2].
Data-centre Opportunity:Data-centre cables currently represent about 9% of domestic revenue and are projected by the MD to double in two years [S2].
Capacity Expansion:The new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant, set to add approximately 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 and is expected to contribute from the second half of FY27 [S2, S3].
Cost Pass-Through Mechanism:Approximately two-thirds of the company's contracts include price-variation clauses, which management expects will allow them to pass on higher copper costs to customers over the next one to two quarters [S2, S3].
Reduced Promoter Pledging:The percentage of promoter holding pledged with lenders has significantly decreased to 4.1% as of June 2026, down from 35% in March 2024 [S4, S8].
Institutional Investor Interest:Foreign Institutional Investors (FII) increased their holding from 9.2% in June 2025 to 11.8% in June 2026, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DII) increased their holding from 12.7% to 14.6% over the same period [S8].
Growing Exports:Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3].

3.Bear case

Margin Contraction:Operating/EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, mainly due to a sharp rise in copper prices [S1, S3].
Lag in Cost Recovery:While price-variation clauses exist, there is a lag of one to two quarters before increased raw material costs can be passed on to customers [S2].
Increasing Receivable Days:Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, primarily due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2].
GST Demand Order:The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. While the company believes it has a strong case and plans to appeal, the financial impact remains a potential risk until resolved [S7].
Historical Governance Concerns:In March 2024, promoter pledging had risen to 35% of their holding, which was seen as a risk for minority shareholders at the time [S4]. Although the current pledged percentage is much lower (4.1% as of June 2026) [S8], it highlights past concerns.

4.Open questions

Revenue Discrepancy:There is a significant difference in reported Q1 FY27 Revenue from Operations, with a news article stating ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY) [S1] and the company's own press release reporting ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) [S3]. Clarification is needed on the correct figure.
Receivable Impact:The impact of increased receivable days on the company's working capital and overall cash flow, despite the CFO stating no defaults have been seen [S2].
Net Debt to Equity Date:The exact reporting date for the stated "Net debt to equity of 0.35 times" [S2] is not specified, making it difficult to assess its most recent relevance.
Company Identity Clarification:It is unclear if "Sarvottam Cable Network," which was penalized ₹8 lakh by the Nagpur Municipal Corporation for illegal wiring [S6], is the same entity as Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE). This could be a point of confusion or reputational risk if not clarified.
